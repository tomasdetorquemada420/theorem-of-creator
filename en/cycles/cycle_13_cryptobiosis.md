### 🧬 MOLECULAR MECHANISM: TREHALOSE + IDP + DSUP

**1. Trehalose (α,α-1,1-glucoside) — "Sugar of Life":**
```
Water leaves → Trehalose forms H-bonds with polar groups of lipids/proteins
  ↓
Replaces hydration shell → preserves membrane fluid phase (prevents gel transition)
  ↓
At full desiccation → vitrification (amorphous solid glass, Tg ≈ 120°C)
  ↓
Molecules frozen in space → no diffusion → no degradation reactions
```

**Critical Concentration:** ~0.5-1 M inside cell for vitrification at anhydrobiosis.

**2. IDP (CAHS, SAHS, MAHS, RvLEAM) — "Protein Mattresses":**
- **CAHS** (Cytoplasmic Abundant Heat Soluble) — cytoplasm, fibrillates in dry state → skeleton
- **SAHS** (Secretory Abundant Heat Soluble) — extracellular space / membrane surface
- **MAHS** (Mitochondrial Abundant Heat Soluble) — mitochondria
- **RvLEAM** (Late Embryogenesis Abundant Mitochondrial) — mitochondria

**Mechanism:**
```
Unstructured in water → form amorphous network on drying (not amyloids!)
  ↓
Physically separate proteins/organelles → prevent aggregation
  ↓
On rehydration → instantly dissolve, no chaperones needed
```

**3. Dsup (Damage Suppressor) — "DNA Shield":**
- Only in tardigrades (Ramazzottius varieornatus, Hypsibius exemplaris)
- Binds nucleosomes → wraps chromatin
- Protection from: ionizing radiation (1000 Gy — human lethal 4-6 Gy), UV, desiccation
- When expressed in human cells (HEK293) → 40-50% reduction in X-ray DNA damage

### 🧪 NEMATODES AND OTHER PAUSE MASTERS

**Caenorhabditis elegans — Dauer Larva:**
- Not full cryptobiosis, but **diapause** (metabolism ~5-10% normal)
- Duration: months instead of weeks of normal life
- Trigger: crowding (pheromones), food deficit, high T
- Pathway: IIS (insulin) → DAF-16/FOXO → stress resistance genes
- **Parallel:** long-lived (naked mole-rats, bats) use same pathways (FOXO, SIRT, AMPK)

**Bdelloid Rotifers:**
- 80 million years **without sexual reproduction** (ameiotic)
- Anhydrobiosis as lifestyle
- Horizontal gene transfer (up to 10% genome — bacteria, fungi, plants)
- Dsup-like proteins, powerful HR DNA repair

**Soil Cyanobacteria (Nostoc, Chroococcidiopsis):**
- Akinetes (spores) — dry, resistant form
- Survive Atacama Desert, Antarctica, ISS (EXPOSE-R2, 531 days)
- Photosynthesis resumes within hours of rewetting
